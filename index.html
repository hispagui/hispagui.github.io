About

Hi, I'm Loris, a mathematician based in Brussels.
I grew up between Lagos, Paris, Brazzaville, Maputo and Brussels, and somewhere along the way I fell for mathematics. I fell hard enough to win the Southern African division of the French Maths Olympiad in 2018, and to spend my master's asking what a "loop" means in a space where you can't draw one!

If I had to sum myself up in one belief, it would be this: I truly believe anyone can learn anything.
Maybe not overnight, and rarely without a few stumbles, but with a little curiosity and a good dose of stubbornness, almost nothing is out of reach. And whenever I need a reminder of that, there's William Blake, Andrew Wiles, Ada Lovelace, ... they are proof that curiosity has no borders. If you have a film, a painting or a topic I should discover next, I’d love to hear it.
